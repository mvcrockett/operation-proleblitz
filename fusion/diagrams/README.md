# Diagrams

These four diagrams replace the five diagram plates at the front of Victoria Crockett's design document. The originals were triaged not usable in FACT-CHECK-LOG.md because they were illegible as rendered, with white on white text, clipped labels, and labels drawn over each other. The redraws below are built only from her text documents. Every element traces to a source named under each diagram. Nothing was invented to fill a gap, and where her documents disagree or run out, the diagram says so.

Every diagram carries the same caption: conceptual framework diagram of the Crockett Reactor, an unproven design by Mary-Victoria Crockett, not an engineering blueprint, no prototype has been built.

## crockett-reactor-overview.svg

The four-layer nested architecture in cross section: outer vacuum chamber, FLiBe blanket shell, vacuum gap, levitated HTS magnetic bottle, plasma core, with the external HTS magnet array, one pellet injector and its trajectory, and neutron arrows. Traces to Section 2 of the full design document and Section 4.2 of her DOE proposal. The geometry follows the Section 2 text, which puts the blanket just inside the chamber wall with the gap between the blanket and the bottle. Two source problems are noted on the diagram itself: the blanket is called a spherical shell inside a chamber described as a cylinder, and the dimension stack does not close. A third is flagged rather than drawn: the levitated bottle itself sits in the neutron path, which her zero embrittlement argument does not address. See OPEN-QUESTIONS.md.

## pellet-cross-section.svg

The four-layer fuel pellet: outer ablative coating, timing delay shell, optional catalyst layer, and the D-T fuel payload, with the stated size and injection speed specs. Traces to Section 3.1 and Section 3.3 of the full design document and the core ignition slides of the explainer deck. The catalyst layer is labelled with its open problem, that no basis for a boron or lithium reactivity boost in a D-T burn is given in her documents.

## closed-loop-flow.svg

The operating cycle as a flow: pellet fabrication, core injection, inside-out ignition, sustained burn, neutrons crossing the gap, then the split into tritium breeding and heat capture, with extracted tritium returning to pellet fabrication and heat running out to the grid. Traces to Section 6 and Schematic 4 of the full design document. The projected breeding ratio is labelled unverified on the diagram because no supporting calculation exists in the corpus.

## heat-extraction-loop.svg

The power conversion chain at her pilot scale: plasma core, FLiBe blanket, primary loop temperatures, intermediate heat exchanger, sCO2 Brayton turbine, and grid output, with her auxiliary load table summarised underneath. Traces to Section 5.4, Schematic 3, and the auxiliary power table of the full design document. Outputs and efficiency are labelled as her projections.
